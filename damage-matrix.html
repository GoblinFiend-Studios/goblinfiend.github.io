Other notes:

Pure damage bypasses this matrix entirely — always deals full, unmodified damage, no matrix lookup, no crit roll, no interaction with Rend.

Rend cancels physical resistance (Slash / Pierce / Crush / Siege) down to a floor of 0 — it strips existing resistance away, it cannot push a value further into vulnerability.
